Pooja Room & Vastu Interior Ideas for Indian Homes

May 12, 2026 4 min read By Siddhivinayak Team

A pooja room is the spiritual sanctuary of an Indian home. Designing it according to Vastu Shastra principles creates positive energy while blending harmoniously with modern apartment aesthetics.

1. Auspicious Mandir Placement (North-East Corner)

According to Vastu Shastra, the North-East (Ishan) corner of your home is the most sacred direction for a pooja space, channeling maximum solar and magnetic energy.

2. Backlit CNC Wooden Jali Screens

Installing intricate backlit CNC laser-cut wooden screens with Om or lotus motifs creates a divine, ambient glow that elevates the sacred feel of the mandir.

3. Marble Altars & Brass Accent Hardware

White Italian marble countertops paired with solid brass bells and traditional oil lamp holders add timeless elegance and warmth to modern apartment pooja units.
