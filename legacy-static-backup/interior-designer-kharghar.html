CIDCO Approvals & Structural Safety Guidelines

Renovating a home in Kharghar involves compliance with local building guidelines. The City and Industrial Development Corporation (CIDCO) and local cooperative housing societies (CHS) have strict guidelines regarding structural changes, wall demolitions, and heavy civil modifications. At Siddhivinayak Designers, we take the stress out of this paperwork:

  • Architectural Drawings: We prepare detailed civil modification and electrical floor plans certified by licensed structural engineers.
  • CIDCO Submissions: We help draft the required stability reports and submit layouts for necessary CIDCO safety clearances.
  • Society Clearances: Our project managers coordinate with society management committees to follow entry security protocols, debris disposal systems, and restricted work hours.

High-Rise Monsoon Waterproofing & Dust Control

Kharghar’s proximity to hills (such as the Kharghar Hills and Pandavkada falls) means properties face high wind pressures and heavy horizontal rain during the monsoons. High-rise flats in Adhiraj Samyama or Sai World Empire are particularly prone to micro-leakages. We use specialized waterproofing solutions: external joint chemical injections, Dr. Fixit polymer membranes in wet zones (bathrooms, balconies, kitchens), and weather-coat external painting. Additionally, since Kharghar is developing rapidly, we incorporate dust-sealing profile systems on custom wardrobes and doors to keep your home pristine.
